Privacy Policy
Last updated: February 26, 2026

Introduction
This Privacy Policy applies to the game VR Paddle Master. We value your privacy and want to be transparent about how we handle your information.

Data Collection
To provide core gameplay features and platform integration, VR Paddle Master processes specific user data through the Meta Horizon/Quest platform.
The game uses the following platform features:

In-App Purchases (IAP): The game allows one-time purchases managed through the Meta Quest platform. To process these transactions and grant access to digital content, the following data is processed:
 In this process, Meta may collect and process:
  - Oculus user ID
  - Purchase history
  - Transaction metadata
  - App-specific entitlement data

Cloud Storage: To allow users to synchronize progress across devices, game data and player statistics are stored via Meta’s cloud services.
This involves:
  - Device identifiers (to ensure hardware compatibility)
  - App save files (scores, progress, settings)
  - Meta User ID (to associate progress with your account)
  These files are stored on the user’s device and may be synced to Meta’s servers.

Internet Access and Permissions
The game requires Internet access solely to enable the platform features mentioned above. No data is transmitted to external third-party servers outside of the Meta platform ecosystem.
The game connects to the internet to:
  - Validate and enable in-app purchases.
  - Sync cloud save data

Analytics
We may access aggregated and anonymized analytics provided by Meta through the Meta Developer Dashboard.
This may include:
  - Total number of users
  - Regional distribution (by country)
  - Device types and OS versions
  - Application session duration and performance metrics

Data Deletion 
We respect your rights regarding your data. If you wish to request the deletion of your user data or game-related information, please contact us at ravensrosedev@gmail.com.
Upon receiving your request and verifying your identity, we will coordinate with Meta's platform services to ensure the removal of your data within our control, in compliance with Meta’s data retention policies.
These analytics do not include any personal user data or identifiable information, and are used solely to improve the application and understand general usage trends.

Children’s Privacy
VR Paddle Master complies with Meta’s age requirements for VR usage. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. 
Any data processed (such as save files or purchases) is managed entirely by the Meta platform in accordance with their safety and privacy policies for younger users.

Data Responsibility
The developer processes data strictly to provide the services requested by the user (purchases and saves). For more details on how Meta handles your information, please refer to the Meta Privacy Policy.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy in the future. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new revision date.
